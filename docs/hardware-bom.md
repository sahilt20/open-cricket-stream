## What you do *not* need

- **Capture card** — phone streams over IP, no HDMI in the chain.
- **Dedicated camera** — modern phone cameras (1080p30) match what FrogBox actually outputs.
- **Cloud encoder** — the Pi handles it.

## Phone

Any iPhone from XR onwards or any Android with a decent rear camera (Pixel 6+, Galaxy S20+, etc.). Use the team's existing phone or a cheap second-hand one dedicated to the rig.

## Recurring costs

Zero, by design. The 4G SIM is a sunk-cost decision per match (PAYG) or a fixed monthly fee depending on the deal. Everything else is software running on hardware you own.
